THE COMPOSITIONS

One playlist, seven finished compositions — 18 minutes, 41 seconds of R&B and neo-soul by Byron Jupiter, recorded at The Box Studios in New Orleans. Every composition below is available to license, and every phonogram also works as a score.

Nº 01

Heartfelt (so-so kicks)

Soul worn openly, drums kept humble — a composition that leads with its heart and stays out of the singer's way.

As a score: the letter finally read aloud; reconciliation; sincerity that has to feel true.

Nº 02

What if

The question every great hook asks — built for the verse that wonders and the chorus that answers.

As a score: the crossroads cue; second thoughts; the road not taken.

Nº 03

Coffee

Morning-after warmth with a slow burn — smooth enough for the slow jam, awake enough to move.

As a score: first light, kitchens and cafés; the rituals that carry a relationship.

Nº 04

Hey What About You

A call across the room that turns into a conversation — playful bounce with room for two voices.

As a score: the reunion, the unexpected call; two people finding the thread again.

Nº 05

Stoop to

Porch-level soul from New Orleans — low, close, and unhurried, with the neighborhood in its pocket.

As a score: the block, the porch, coming home; a sense-of-place cue.

Nº 06

Plenty of Phases 2

One relationship, every season of it — a composition that changes its weather and keeps its soul.

As a score: time passing, chapters turning; montage and series arcs.

Nº 07

Dead Silence

What isn't said, said loudest — sparse, heavy space for the vocal that carries the weight alone.

As a score: the pause after the news; aftermath; grief held quiet.
